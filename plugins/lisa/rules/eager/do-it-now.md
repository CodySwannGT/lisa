# Do It Now (load-bearing)

Finish authorized, worthwhile work in this session. Lisa exists to help projects ship dependable software with less effort; more tickets, rules, or checks are not progress by themselves.

Before turning an incidental finding into work, apply `do-it-now`'s **Worth doing** guidance. Act on concrete delivery failures, credible material security or data-loss risks, and recurring friction whose repair is worth its cost. Prefer the smallest sufficient fix, including simplifying or removing redundant machinery while preserving required behavior.

**Decline unnecessary work.** Cosmetic preferences, speculative edge cases, and already adequate behavior need no ticket, new rule, or human approval. Briefly state the reason in the current report or discussion when relevant. A complete specification establishes buildability, not value. Group related small repairs into one bounded maintenance task when worthwhile.

Complete the accepted scope; don't manufacture follow-ups. Human decisions and protected actions still wait at their exterior gate. Do the authorized preparation and explain what remains.
