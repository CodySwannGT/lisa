## Policy (single source for every task)

**Default: an agent performs every act it has access to perform.** A human gate exists only when the agent genuinely lacks access to the act, and the gate's text must name that access. The closed list:

1. interactive SSO / OAuth / MFA login;
2. creating a credential in a vendor UI, or writing a secret store the agent cannot reach;
3. spending money (purchases, paid plans, paid-account upgrades);
4. a legal signature (contract, BAA, DPA, license acceptance on behalf of the company);
5. an action only a person's account can take (an account only a person holds, including a protected-deployment required reviewer or a ruleset approval that must come from a person);
6. a hold the operator explicitly asked for (the operator's own hold, as an existing behaviour).

Everything else is decided and performed by the agent, with the reasoning recorded on the work item so a person can reverse it later. That covers product, scoping or design decisions, risk acceptance, a compliance judgement, naming an accountable owner from available evidence, promoting or demoting learnings, harness interventions, automation retirement, exploratory-QA significance, picking an epic or parent, dependency ratification, and threshold-ratchet exceptions.

**Stricter switch:** `humanGate.mode`, `"access-only"` (default) | `"strict"`. `strict` restores the pre-#4687 behaviour:
- the legacy acts (`accountable-person`, compliance sign-off, `lisa-policy`) count as human gates again;
- the defaults of `dependencies.humanSignOff` and `learnings.lowConfidence` go back to `"default"` and `"human-triage"`.

Under `access-only` those defaults are `"none"` and `"auto-merge"`. A key the project sets explicitly always wins over the mode. `humanGate.humanOnly` still widens the gate in either mode.

**Authorship rule unchanged:** surfaces declared human-authored (a host's `PROJECT_RULES.md`, `AGENTS.md`) still receive no automated writes. That is an authorship-separation rule, not an access gate. Promotion tickets to other destinations file build-ready under `access-only`.
